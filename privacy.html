Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

1. Who we are

UCG Deals ("the app") is a private content tool operated by the owner of the UCG Deals TikTok account (see Imprint). It is used only by its operator and is not offered to the public.

2. What data the app processes

When the operator connects their own TikTok account via TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • an access token and refresh token issued by TikTok,
  • basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) as permitted by the granted scopes,
  • status information about uploads made by the app.

The app does not collect data from other TikTok users, viewers or customers.

3. Purpose

The data is used solely to upload photo slideshows and videos as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account and to check the upload status.

4. Storage

Tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer. Images that are published to this website for TikTok to fetch are removed after the upload is complete. No data is sold or shared with third parties, except TikTok as required to perform the upload.

5. Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). GitHub may process technical access data such as IP addresses when you visit this site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

6. Your rights

You can revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok settings under "Security and permissions → Apps and services". You may request information about, correction or deletion of your data using the contact details in the Imprint. Under the GDPR you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Datenschutzerklärung (Kurzfassung)

UCG Deals ist ein privates Tool, das ausschließlich vom Betreiber des TikTok-Accounts UCG Deals genutzt wird. Verarbeitet werden nur die Zugangsdaten (Tokens) und Basis-Profildaten des eigenen TikTok-Accounts, um Beiträge als Entwurf hochzuladen. Tokens werden lokal auf dem Rechner des Betreibers gespeichert, Bilder auf dieser Website werden nach dem Upload gelöscht. Es werden keine Daten Dritter erhoben und keine Daten verkauft. Die Website wird über GitHub Pages gehostet. Der Zugriff kann jederzeit in den TikTok-Einstellungen widerrufen werden. Kontakt und Anfragen zu Auskunft oder Löschung: siehe Impressum.

UCG Deals

UCG Deals is a small content tool used by the operator of the UCG Deals TikTok account. It helps prepare photo slideshows and short videos about products we recommend as a TikTok Shop affiliate, and uploads them as drafts to our own TikTok account. Every post is reviewed and published manually in the TikTok app.
